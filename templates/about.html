About MedWaste Map

Making safe medicine disposal information easier to understand.

Our Project

MedWaste Map is an educational website designed to help people understand the importance of safely disposing of expired and unwanted medicines.

Many people are unsure what to do with unused medicines. Incorrect disposal may contribute to environmental pollution or accidental exposure. Our website provides educational information and helps users explore potential collection options.

Problem Statement

Residents may not know how to dispose of expired or unwanted medicines safely. Information about suitable disposal routes and collection facilities can be difficult to find.

Proposed Solution

MedWaste Map provides a central source of disposal guidance, medicine expiry awareness, educational quizzes and collection location search links.

Our Objectives

  • Spread awareness about responsible medicine disposal.
  • Explain common disposal precautions.
  • Help users investigate local collection options.
  • Encourage confirmation of collection services before visiting.
  • Promote environmental responsibility.
