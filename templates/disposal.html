Safe Medicine Disposal Guide

Learn how to handle different types of unwanted medicines.

💊

Tablets and Capsules

Keep them secure and ask a pharmacist or local authority about an accepted medicine take-back option.

🧴

Liquid Medicines

Keep containers closed. Do not pour medicines into sinks or drains unless specific official instructions direct you to do so.

🩹

Creams and Patches

Follow product-specific instructions and seek advice from a pharmacist about suitable disposal.

💉

Needles and Sharps

Never put loose needles in ordinary waste or recycling. Ask a healthcare provider about approved sharps disposal.

🔒

Safe Temporary Storage

Keep unwanted medicines secure, away from children and pets, until a suitable disposal route is arranged.

📦

Packaging

Check local rules before recycling packaging. Remove or obscure personal prescription details where appropriate.

⚠️ Important Safety Advice

  • Do not share expired or unwanted medicines.
  • Do not routinely flush medicines down the toilet.
  • Prefer an officially accepted take-back service when available.
  • Ask a pharmacist if the correct disposal method is unclear.
