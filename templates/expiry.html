Medicine Expiry Awareness

Understand expiry dates and responsible medicine storage.

What Is an Expiry Date?

The expiry date is the date up to which a medicine is expected to meet its specifications when stored according to its approved conditions.

Why Should You Check Expiry Dates?

  • Expired medicines may no longer meet their expected quality.
  • Some products have specific storage requirements.
  • Opened products may have a shorter usable period.
  • Checking dates helps identify medicines needing review.

Medicine Storage Tips

  • Follow the storage instructions on the label.
  • Keep medicines in their original labelled packaging where possible.
  • Protect medicines from heat, moisture and direct sunlight as directed.
  • Keep medicines away from children and pets.
  • Ask a pharmacist about products with unclear labels or dates.
Remember: Do not use a medicine simply because it looks normal. Follow its expiry date and product-specific instructions, and ask a healthcare professional if unsure.
