Verification Notice

A pharmacy or healthcare facility is not automatically a medicine collection centre. Contact the facility to confirm whether it accepts unwanted medicines, which items it accepts, and when the service is available.

Before Visiting a Collection Centre

  • Confirm that medicine collection is currently offered.
  • Ask which types of medicines are accepted.
  • Confirm the address and operating hours.
  • Check whether special handling is required.
